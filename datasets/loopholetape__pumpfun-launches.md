# pump.fun launches with outcomes

> **(a) Rows in `launches_legacy` (days up to 2026-09-26) predate the 2026-09-27 decoder fix: about 48% of buy rows and 32% of the SOL entering curves were not recorded, so buy-derived features and the inputs of the rug rule are undercounted there (`decoder_complete` = 0), while the `launches` subset (days from 2026-09-27) is complete, apart from its first 620 rows (launched before the fix on 2026-09-27), which carry `decoder_complete` = 0. (b) Early features exist on 78.8% of the launches, and that half is rugged on 66.0% of resolved classic launches against 43.0% for the rest, so never model the sampled rows as the population. (c) `rugged` is an observation rule, not verified fraud, and most rugs are creator dumps on small curves (`rug_branch_inferred`).**
>
> **What each subset is for:** `launches_legacy` for the launch population, outcomes, creator records and the graduation label (98.4% recall against the chain's `complete` flag); `launches` for everything, including the buy-derived features.

One row per pump.fun token launch that the project's own Solana recorder saw between 2026-08-31 04:03:25 UTC and 2026-10-27 23:59:58 UTC (58 UTC days, 2,331,945 launches), with what happened to the launch afterwards: whether it graduated to a PumpSwap pool, whether the project's rug rule fired, when, and how high the bonding curve got. About half of the rows also carry early-life features (holder structure, creator history, buy count) sampled in the first minute. The launches are split into two subsets by the state of the recorder: `launches_legacy` holds the days up to 2026-09-26, recorded before the decoder fix (859,208 rows); `launches` holds the days from 2026-09-27, recorded with the complete decoder (1,472,737 rows), and is the default configuration. A further config, `graduation_pools`, holds the first 30 minutes of 39,967 PumpSwap pools that graduations opened between 2026-09-23 11:10:21 UTC and 2026-10-27 23:59:06 UTC.

The data are facts about public on-chain activity, not advice. They also have defects. The table under "Known issues" lists them and KNOWN_ISSUES.md explains each one with numbers. Read it before you train anything on this.

**Need it live?** These files are the copy that is at least 7 days old. The same recorder answers now, free and without a key: [new launches ranked by rug and graduation odds](https://api.loopholetape.com/v1/radar?src=hf), [every graduation of the last 24 hours with what its pool did in the next 30 minutes](https://api.loopholetape.com/v1/graduations/public?src=hf) and [a check for any Solana token](https://api.loopholetape.com/v1/token_check?src=hf). For agents there is an MCP server at `https://api.loopholetape.com/mcp`. More under "The live service" below.

## How it was recorded

The recorder is the project's own: a Yellowstone gRPC stream of the pump.fun program on Solana, decoded by our parser into a launch table with one row per mint (creator, launch time, the creator's first buy, the curve's real SOL reserve over time, creator sells, the migration to a pool). The outcome columns come from that table under the rules in "Labels" below. The columns described as coming from the curve account were read from the chain itself: the bonding-curve account of every launch was read between 2026-09-30 15:07:29 UTC and 2026-10-31 06:51:49 UTC, which gives what the curve is quoted in, mayhem mode, the holder-reward flag, the creator fee and the chain's own `complete` flag. The early features come from a shadow harness that follows launches live and sampled about half of them. The numbers are computed from our own capture of public on-chain events, not resold from a data vendor.

## Labels

The labels follow rule version `outcome-1`, published live at https://api.loopholetape.com/v1/labels. In short:

- `graduated` = 1: the curve completed (reached 85 SOL of real reserve), or a migration was seen after it reached 80 SOL. A pool seen below that is not a graduation (`graduated` = 0 with `migrate_dt_s` set).
- `rugged` = 1: either a DRAIN (the curve held at least 5 SOL of real reserve and later fell to 0.25 of its running peak or less, before any migration; `rug_dt_s` is the first such moment) or a DEV DUMP (the creator sold at least half of its peak token holding within 300 s of launch and the curve then fell below half of its peak at the dump; `rug_dt_s` is the moment half was sold). `rugged` and `graduated` are not exclusive (KI-13). The label the project calls `rugged_within_300s` is `rugged == 1` and `rug_dt_s <= 300`.
- `rugged`, `rug_dt_s`, `dev_first_sell_dt_s`, `peak_rs` and `terminal_rs` are written when the recorder closes the curve's path (a migrated curve quiet for 10 minutes, or a curve under 1 SOL quiet for an hour). A path that was never closed reads null, never a provisional 0 (KI-08).
- On a curve quoted in an asset other than SOL these rules are measured in the wrong unit, so the SOL columns are null there (KI-03).

**Label check.** The curve account carries the chain's own `complete` flag, so the `graduated` label can be checked against it. On the 1,355,895 classic launches of this release (SOL-quoted, not mayhem, curve account read): the chain says complete on 42,480, we labelled 41,990 as graduated, 41,921 are in both. Recall 98.68% (559 completed curves not labelled), precision 99.84% (69 labelled launches the chain does not call complete). The live schema document reports recall 98.71% and precision 99.81% over all 64 day files (1,465,042 classic launches). Recall is below 98.5% on 2026-09-01 (97.4%), 2026-09-02 (89.5%), 2026-09-03 (94.8%), 2026-09-04 (97.2%), 2026-09-15 (97.8%), 2026-09-26 (97.9%), 2026-10-05 (97.6%), 2026-10-11 (98.0%), 2026-10-21 (97.8%), 2026-10-23 (97.5%); every other day is 98.5% or higher. Per-day table in KNOWN_ISSUES.md, KI-09.

## Config `features`

What the project's API computed, minute by minute, for the pump.fun mints it was watching, joined afterwards to what happened to each launch: 4,440,896 rows, 1,122,565 mints counted per day file, 34 files (one per UTC day of rows, 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-27), 187 MB, 66 columns.

**What a row is.** Once a minute the API's minute logger scores every candidate mint with the same code that answers its checks and keeps one row per mint: the ten model inputs as computed at that moment (`f_*`), the flags observed so far (`flag_*`), the numbers the API would have published at that moment (`p_rug_within_300s`, `p_true_graduation`) and the rug model's raw score. The rows are not a log of answered requests; they are what a check at that moment would have answered. A launch gets a row under 30 s only when a tick falls inside its first 30 s (`window` = young: 782,885 rows, 49.4% of the 1,584,881 launches of 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-27 in our launch table, a random half by the tick's phase); from 30 s to 5 min it gets one every minute and from 5 to 15 min one every 5 minutes, only while our capture had counted at least 5 buys (`window` = older: 3,658,011 rows). Mayhem launches and mints that already carry a flag are included. Nothing in the feature columns was recomputed from the tape: each row is one stored row, except that 92 byte-identical repeats within a tick were dropped and the repair mask below was applied; `capture_lag_s` is joined by time from the recorder's own lag log.

**Outcomes and the leakage rule.** The outcome columns come from the launch table that also builds the `launches` config, joined by mint and read at `labels_as_of` (2026-10-15T02:22:12Z to 2026-10-31T07:30:51Z for these files), always at least 72 hours after the launch; a launch younger than that at the read is `outcome_pending` with null outcomes, and a day joins this config only when it is 7 or more days old, so no row here is pending. Every feature column is computed from the stored row alone (a test builds one day against two different outcome tables and the feature columns come out identical). `rug_within_300s_after_row` is the API's own target at the row (the label its rug model was fitted on): 1 when the launch rugged after the row and within 300 s of it. Checked on every row of these files: 0 rows with `t` at or after `labels_as_of`; 0 settled rows read under 72 hours after their launch; 0 rows labelled 1 whose rug is not after the row. 2,570,984 rows were sampled after their launch's rug and 67,945 after its migration: their flags and `f_rs` already show the outcome, so for prediction keep `rugged_before_row` = 0, `migrated_before_row` = 0 and `repaired` = false. Rows of one mint are not independent: split by mint or by time, never by row.

**The repair mask.** From the store's first row (2026-09-23 20:14 UTC) until the fix on 2026-09-29 (05:52 UTC), a curve that had completed was read as drained: true graduates sampled after their completion carried `curve_drained` and a graduation number at the model's floor (0.0041) or a stale value. The project measured the defect and listed the 7,567 affected store rows; the 7,449 of them inside these files have `curve_drained` removed and `p_true_graduation` nulled, `repaired` = true, and `repair_reason` says what changed (the others are rows of 2026-09-23, a partial day that is not in this config).

**Outcome status of the rows:** settled 3,819,554, not SOL-quoted (SOL outcomes null by rule) 564,946, unresolved 56,303, no launch row 93, pending 0.

## Update policy

Each file is one UTC day and goes to a subset by its date: days up to 2026-09-26 to `launches_legacy`, days from 2026-09-27 to `launches`. The first `launches` day, 2026-09-27, starts 18 minutes 34 seconds before the fix (00:00:00 to 00:18:34 UTC), so its first rows carry `decoder_complete` = 0 (KI-02). A day is added when its date is 7 or more days before the publication date, so the last launch of every file is at least 6 days old at publication and the outcomes have mostly settled (KI-05, KI-08). Days are added as they reach that age, at no fixed time of day. The `graduation_pools` config grows with every publication: each added day brings its pools (the recorder's store began on 2026-09-23 at 11:10 UTC, so the pools of this release cover 828.8 hours). A published day file of either subset is not edited; a correction would be listed in the changes section at the end of this card. The newest 7 days are not here: the API at https://api.loopholetape.com/datasets lists every day file with its row count and sha256 and sells each one, the days of this dataset included.

## Quickstart

With pandas and pyarrow, from the folder of this repository:

```python
import glob
import numpy as np
import pandas as pd

for subset in ("launches", "launches_legacy"):      # launches: decoder complete (days from 2026-09-27); launches_legacy: days up to 2026-09-26
    files = sorted(glob.glob(f"data/{subset}/*.parquet"))
    if not files:
        continue
    df = pd.concat((pd.read_parquet(f) for f in files), ignore_index=True)
    kind = np.select([df["quote_asset"].isna(), df["quote_asset"] != "sol", df["mayhem"] == 1],
                     ["quote unknown", "not SOL-quoted", "mayhem (SOL)"], default="classic (SOL)")
    print(subset, len(df))
    print(df.groupby(kind)["graduated"].agg(rows="size", labelled="count", graduated="sum", rate="mean"))
```

from datasets import load_dataset
